Step‑Up Approach — Necrotizing Pancreatitis (interactive)

Mobile-first, touch-friendly decision tool summarizing the step-up approach. Use for teaching and quick decision checks — not a substitute for full clinical judgment or local protocols.
1) Timing since symptom onset
Goal: delay invasive necrosectomy until walled‑off necrosis if possible (commonly ≥4 weeks).
2) Is infection suspected or proven in necrosis?
Infection = fever, rising WBC, positive cultures, gas on imaging, or clear clinical deterioration.
3) Is the patient clinically unstable / septic or organ‑failure?
4) Response to conservative / drainage therapy
Conservative = antibiotics when indicated, organ support, nutritional support (enteral feeding), and ICU care as needed.
